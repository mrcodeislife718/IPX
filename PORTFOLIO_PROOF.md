# IPX — Portfolio Proof Contract

**Track:** Commercial IP registry / protection platform

IPX is complete only when identity, evidence intake, registration/certificate state, provenance, vaulting, monitoring, and dispute/revocation workflows are implemented securely and can support real IP-holder use cases.

Required proof: end-to-end registration/evidence/certificate flows; tests for identity, ownership claims, scope, conflicts, version history, revocation, and auditability; adversarial tests for forged evidence, impersonation, tampering, replay, and cross-account access; benchmarks for verification/lookup and operational throughput; security/privacy and evidence integrity; deployment; real users; paid registrations/add-ons and retention where commercialized.

**Next proof target:** run a cohort of controlled IP records through registration, evidence updates, conflicting claims, verification, revocation, and audit reconstruction while attacking tamper/impersonation paths.
