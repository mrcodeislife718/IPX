# IPX — Portfolio Proof Contract

**Track:** Commercial private modern intellectual-property office

IPX is a private, independent intellectual-property office and technology platform. It is not a government agency and does not claim sovereign authority. Its proof contract covers the complete private IP lifecycle represented by the canonical product: provenance, identity and ownership, search and examination support, filing preparation, private records and certificates, evidence preservation, lifecycle administration, monitoring, commercialization, professional workflows, APIs, and government-facing workflow support where applicable.

## Completion rule

IPX is not complete because a registry, certificate, vault, payment path, or monitoring feature works in isolation. Commercial completion requires the intended customer journey to operate end to end with explicit legal/authority boundaries, durable evidence, security, recovery, auditable financial state, and production operations.

Private IPX records and services must never be represented as statutory grants where only a competent government authority can create the legal effect.

## Core proof surfaces

Required evidence includes:

- identity, organization, inventor/author/contributor, ownership, assignment, delegation, and signature controls;
- evidence intake, timestamps, hashes, chain of custody, version history, conflict handling, and independently verifiable records;
- prior-art/conflict search and examination-support workflows with traceable sources and review history;
- patent/design, trademark, copyright, amendment/response, jurisdiction, deadline, renewal, and government-facing package workflows as implemented;
- private certificate and public-verification behavior with revocation/supersession integrity;
- encrypted evidence preservation, authorization, audit history, backup, restore, and incident recovery;
- Watchdog monitoring lifecycle, configurable catalog/pricing, subscription state, findings, escalation, and cancellation;
- licensing, diligence, assignment/transfer, commercialization, and professional-service workflows;
- API/white-label authorization, tenant isolation, rate limits, auditability, and portability;
- billing, invoice, payment, webhook, refund/dispute, entitlement/fulfillment, and revenue reconciliation where commercialized.

## Adversarial and failure proof

Qualification must exercise forged evidence, impersonation, conflicting ownership claims, replay/tampering, stale or revoked authority, cross-account access, duplicate payment/webhook delivery, payment/provider outage, search/provider failure, partial workflow completion, corrupted persistence, backup/restore, and deadline/notification failure.

## Commercial proof

Repository qualification is distinct from market proof. Commercial evidence should include real customer or professional use, paid transactions or contracts, monitoring retention, fulfillment accuracy, support burden, conversion, renewal/expansion, and unit economics for the services actually sold.

Pricing parity or comparison claims must be tied to the applicable current reference source and effective date. IPX-created services such as Watchdog require explicit configured commercial pricing rather than invented defaults.
