# IPX — IP and Provenance Record

## Ownership position

IPX is independently conceived, architected, directed, and developed by Charles Castillo. The repository history is the canonical technical chronology for the materials committed here.

The original IPX architecture, product design, specifications, workflows, documentation, naming, implementation decisions, and original source committed by the owner are proprietary intellectual property of Charles Castillo unless an individual file expressly states otherwise.

## Development tools

Software-development tools, including AI-assisted coding and reasoning tools, may be used as instruments in the development process. Tool assistance does not by itself create a transfer of ownership or authorship. Repository commits, specifications, architecture records, issue history, qualification artifacts, and signed or timestamped evidence should be preserved as the technical provenance trail.

## Third-party boundary

External packages, libraries, standards, APIs, protocols, datasets, model weights, fonts, media, reference implementations, and other third-party materials remain governed by their respective licenses and ownership. IPX does not claim ownership of those materials.

When third-party material is incorporated, production releases must preserve required notices and license obligations and must not represent third-party work as original IPX technology.

## Provenance controls

IPX repositories should preserve, at minimum:

- Git commit timestamps and immutable commit SHAs;
- author and committer identity metadata;
- tagged release history for production releases;
- architecture and decision records for material system changes;
- dependency manifests and lockfiles where applicable;
- third-party license and notice records;
- machine-verifiable test, benchmark, and qualification evidence where applicable;
- cryptographic hashes or signatures for high-value evidence and release artifacts;
- contributor identity and IP-assignment records before accepting external proprietary contributions.

## No false provenance

A timestamp establishes that a particular repository state existed by that time; it is not, by itself, a patent, copyright registration, trademark registration, or court determination of ownership. IPX documentation must distinguish technical provenance evidence from statutory rights and legal conclusions.

## Commercial IP boundary

Public repository visibility does not waive proprietary rights. Confidential production internals, customer information, credentials, security-sensitive operating material, unpublished inventions, and trade secrets should remain outside public repositories unless intentionally disclosed by the owner.

## Repository authority

For this repository, the canonical production/public record is `main`. Historical branches may be retained for provenance, but no branch may be treated as authoritative unless explicitly designated.
